## What's different about selling a membership

- **Recurring payment and churn.** The sale isn't one purchase. A month-two cancellation is a lost sale, so the numbers to watch are retention and lifetime value, not sign-ups alone.
- **How easy it is to cancel.**
  - Cancelling should be as easy as joining (LAW-17).
  - The DMCC Act's subscription contract rules are **not in force** as of 20 Nov 2026. The government says January 2027, and the draft regulations name 1 January 2027.
  - Re-check before Danger Club launches, then re-rate LAW-17.
- **Joining is a decision about identity and belonging.** People join a club to be a certain kind of person among certain people. That's powerful, which is why the harm-marker audit applies (CUS-09):
  - unaccountable authority;
  - a costly or humiliating exit;
  - control of information;
  - guilt used as a lever;
  - money or recruiting as the real goal.
- **Event tickets on top of the subscription.** Every price shown must include mandatory charges (LAW-03). In October 2026 the CMA fined Gold's Gym £45k for leaving a mandatory joining fee out of its total membership price, so the same goes for any joining fee.
- **Soft pathways into Pete's other ventures.** These must stay genuinely soft. The RGC review's harm markers warn against money or recruiting becoming the real goal of a high-identity group.
