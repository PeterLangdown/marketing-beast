# Hooga Retreats
## Business & Marketing Brief
*November 2026 · Internal reference for marketing, website development and booking-channel strategy*

## 1. The business

Hooga Retreats is an independent experiential accommodation brand based in and around Canterbury, Kent, run by Peter and Natalie. It creates imaginative, private overnight escapes for people who want to spend meaningful time together, celebrate an occasion or break from everyday life.

Hooga operates three individually designed retreats: a Mongolian yurt, a restored showman's carriage and a converted double-decker bus. Each is a destination in its own right, combining distinctive accommodation with activities, atmosphere and spaces that guests can discover and enjoy together.

**Core proposition:** A night away that feels like an adventure — and becomes a memory.

Hooga is not a luxury hotel, a conventional holiday cottage or simply somewhere to sleep while visiting Kent. The accommodation itself is the experience.

## 3. Primary target audience

The marketing priority is couples aged approximately **25–45**, typically living within **90 minutes' travel of Canterbury**. This includes much of Kent and reachable parts of London and the South East; prioritise audiences who can get away without a complicated journey.

### Their motivations

- Celebrating a birthday, anniversary or other meaningful occasion.
- Arranging a surprise for a partner.
- Finding a night or two away from children, work and household responsibilities.
- Reconnecting as a couple and spending undistracted time together.
- Discovering an unusual experience that feels more special than booking an ordinary hotel.
- Giving an experience as a gift rather than buying another possession.

They are buying both an enjoyable stay and the anticipation of it. They want to arrive somewhere that already feels special, with enough to do that they don't have to create an itinerary from scratch.

The decision-maker may be one partner arranging the surprise rather than the couple making a joint holiday decision. Market to the person looking for a thoughtful gift or a memorable date night.

### Secondary audience

Families looking for quality time together, especially at the Midnight Express and Magical Yurt. Their motivation is play, discovery and shared memories. Keep this audience distinct from the central couples-focused midweek campaign.

This is a marketing target, not a blanket restriction on who may book. Clear house rules should manage occupancy, parties, visitors and disturbance.

## 4. What guests are really buying

| Feature | Emotional benefit |
| --- | --- |
| Unusual, lovingly designed spaces | Discovery, novelty and a sense of stepping outside everyday life |
| Private cinemas and cosy lounges | A ready-made date night; uninterrupted time together |
| Outdoor bathing, firelight and countryside views | Intimacy, slowing down and sensory enjoyment |
| Private retreats | Freedom to be together without the social demands of a hotel |
| A self-contained experience | Less planning and decision-making; more time enjoying the stay |

The offer is best understood as an **experience-led micro-break**. Guests can arrive, settle in, enjoy a film, share food, bathe beneath the stars or simply sit by the fire, as applicable to their chosen retreat. They can go out and explore if they wish, but don't need to leave the property to have a satisfying evening.

The value is not formal service, marble finishes or the amenities of the Ritz. It lies in imaginative design, personal touches, privacy, comfort and the pleasure of doing something different.

Guests are invited to engage with the experience at their own pace. Some features, such as wood-fired hot tubs, require preparation and participation; communicate this honestly rather than promising hotel-style convenience or a fully serviced arrival.

## 5. Main commercial objective

Increase occupancy from **Sunday to Thursday**, focusing on one- and two-night local escapes.

- Two nights are the preferred booking: guests have time to enjoy the facilities without rushing, and longer stays reduce changeovers.
- One-night bookings remain welcome, particularly where they fill otherwise empty midweek availability.
- Weekend demand is already strong; the priority is to create new demand during quieter nights rather than discounting peak periods unnecessarily.
- Use occasion-led campaigns and two-night packages or value-added offers to encourage longer stays. Test incentives before adopting permanent discounts.
- Develop Hooga's own direct booking channel alongside Airbnb, Vrbo and Booking.com, where suitable, to reduce dependency on any individual platform.

Measure progress through midweek occupancy, revenue per available night, average length of stay, direct-booking share and acquisition cost.

## 6. Positioning and marketing direction

**Positioning statement:** For couples seeking a special escape close to home, Hooga offers imaginative private retreats where the evening is an experience in itself — a place to reconnect, discover something new and create memories together.

### Key campaign themes

- **The perfect date night:** a film, food, a soak and time together, all in one private retreat.
- **Make the occasion memorable:** birthdays, anniversaries and surprises that feel genuinely personal.
- **A night away from it all:** a break from routine that doesn't require a long journey or extensive planning.
- **Stay a little longer:** two nights to explore, relax and enjoy every part of the experience.

Lead with the imagined evening and the memories guests will make, not a list of facilities. Use photography and short videos that show how the experience unfolds: arriving, discovering the space, sunset, firelight, cinema, bathing and slow mornings.

The voice should be warm, playful, imaginative and personal, with a little magic. Avoid generic hotel language, exaggerated luxury claims or promising a flawless experience. Make practical details clear before booking, including the differing bathing facilities, seasonal extras, guest-operated amenities and occasional local noise.

## 7. Suggested public-facing introduction

Hooga Retreats is a collection of extraordinary little escapes in and around Canterbury. Whether you're celebrating an anniversary, surprising someone you love or simply escaping everyday life for a night or two, we've created spaces where the experience starts the moment you arrive.

From hidden cinemas and starlit baths to fireside evenings and wonderfully unusual places to sleep, every retreat offers something to discover. You don't need a day's worth of plans to make your time away special. Just bring someone you love, settle in and make a memory of your own.

**A little escape. A lovely adventure. A memory you'll keep.**
