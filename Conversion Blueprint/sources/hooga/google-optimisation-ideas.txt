# PROJECT SPECIFICATION: LUXURY COUPLES GLAMPING CONVERSION FUNNEL

## 1. META-GOAL, FRAME & TELOS (READ BEFORE WRITING ANY CODE OR COPY)

### The Telos (Ultimate Purpose)
This project is **NOT** a traditional hospitality brochure website, directory listing, or multi-page brand exploration site. Its sole telos is to operate as a **closed-loop, high-ticket direct-response sales funnel** that converts paid Google PPC intent traffic (£2–£5+ per click) into £400–£900+ luxury romantic stays and open-date experience gift vouchers with zero traffic leakage.

### The Buyer Frame & Psychological Context
* **Target Audience:** Couples aged 25–45 living within a 90-minute drive/train radius of Canterbury, Kent (heavily weighted toward Greater London and the affluent South East).
* **Core Buying Trigger:** Almost nobody is buying "glamping" or "a tent." They are buying an **emotional milestone or relational sanctuary**—specifically **Wedding Anniversaries**, **Milestone Birthdays**, or **Rare Child-Free Nights Away**.
* **The Core Conversion Friction to Solve:** Roughly half of occasion buyers are planning a *surprise* for their partner or haven't arranged childcare/annual leave yet. Forcing every visitor into a rigid calendar picker kills conversions. Therefore, the architecture must give equal visual weight to **Path A (Lock Exact Dates)** and **Path B (Buy an Open-Date Named Experience Voucher)**.

### Non-Negotiable Architectural Laws (Anti-Goals)
1. **Zero Navigation Leaks:** Do NOT build a standard header navigation bar, multi-page menu, blog feed, footer sitemap, or outbound social media icon row. Paid visitors leave the page only by purchasing, claiming a lead magnet, or closing the browser tab.
2. **Mobile Commuter First:** Assume 75%+ of visitors are scrolling one-handed on an iPhone over patchy 4G (e.g., commuting out of London). Every interaction must be thumb-friendly, sub-2-second fast, and completable via Apple Pay or Google Pay in under 60 seconds.
3. **Outcomes Over Specs:** Never lead with technical unit specifications or camp rules. Lead with sensory arrival, seclusion, warmth, romance, and effortless proximity to London, backed by transparent pricing and risk-free date changes.
4. **Attribution Integrity:** Paid Smart Bidding lives or dies on clean data. No UI component, iframe, or redirect may strip URL tracking parameters (`gclid`, `gbraid`, `wbraid`, `utm_*`) between landing and payment confirmation.
